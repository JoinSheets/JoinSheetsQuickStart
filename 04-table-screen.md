Click **Apply and Open Table** in the bottom-left corner of the screen --
this starts the build and automatically opens the **Table** screen.

From here it's all familiar ground: the icons in the column headers give
you search, quick filtering, and sorting. The **Filter** button in the
header opens a panel with the advanced settings -- filtering, grouping,
and multi-level sorting -- and the **Export** button opens the panel for
exporting to Excel or CSV. Exactly what you see on screen goes into the
file, filters, sorting, and hidden columns included.
