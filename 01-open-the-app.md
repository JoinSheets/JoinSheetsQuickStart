The fastest way to get a first look at the app's interface is to open the
[live demo](/demo/) on the site. It opens right in your browser and already
comes with a demo dataset.

To work with your own data locally, download the
[JoinSheets](https://github.com/JoinSheets/JoinSheets) repository and open
`index.html`.

On first launch, the app finds the data (by default, the demo dataset
directory `sample-data/`), applies the basic preset that ships with it, and
opens straight to the **Table** screen. So you'll see the app actually
working the very first time you open it, without configuring anything
yourself.

{{% tip %}}
You can switch the interface language and color theme at any time from the
**Settings** panel (the gear icon, top right). The app's interface language
and the language of this documentation are independent of each other.
{{% /tip %}}
